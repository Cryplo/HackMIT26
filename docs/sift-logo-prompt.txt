Use case: logo-brand
Create a refined, distinctive logo for "Sift", a reimbursement review application that turns receipts and supporting evidence into clear, trustworthy review decisions.
Deliver one finished horizontal logo lockup: a compact symbol on the left, the exact word "Sift" on the right. White background, dark forest green wordmark, symbol using forest green and a restrained fresh mint-green accent. Large logo centered with generous whitespace. Flat vector-like graphic, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text, no tagline, no variations sheet.
Symbol concept: three simple short horizontal evidence bars pass through a subtle open geometric frame and resolve into one clean aligned bar, with negative space subtly suggesting an S. Make it feel like sorting and clarity; avoid a literal funnel, magnifying glass, shield, checkmark, money sign, leaf, or generic AI sparkles. Symbol must remain recognizable at small sizes and work in one color. Use very few shapes, balanced optical weight, soft but precise corners.
Wordmark: beautifully drawn modern humanist sans-serif lettering, exact capitalization "Sift", confident and approachable, carefully kerned, distinctive but highly legible. The mark and lettering should feel like one cohesive brand. Professional fintech software identity without looking like a bank. Do not add any existing company logos.
